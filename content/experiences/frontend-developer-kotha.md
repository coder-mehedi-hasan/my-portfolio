## About the Company

Kotha Apps & Technologies Ltd is an app development company focused on building polished, user-friendly mobile and web applications. The team cared a lot about design quality and attention to detail in every screen.

## What I Did

I worked as a frontend developer, turning designs into responsive, interactive interfaces.

- Built reusable UI components and page flows for client applications.
- Collaborated closely with designers to translate Figma mockups pixel-perfectly.
- Optimized page load performance and animation smoothness.

## What I Learned

- **Design craft:** working beside designers taught me how spacing, typography, and motion affect how a product feels.
- **Component architecture:** I learned to build a component library that stayed consistent and maintainable as the app grew.
- **Accessibility:** adding keyboard navigation and proper semantics became second nature.

## Why It Mattered

This role shaped how I think about the frontend. It gave me an eye for detail and a strong foundation in building interfaces that are both beautiful and accessible.
