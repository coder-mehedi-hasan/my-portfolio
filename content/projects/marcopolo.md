## Project Scope

Marcopolo is a comprehensive hotel management system that brings core operational workflows into one product, including inventory, human resources, accounting, payroll, and management processes.

## My Role

I contributed to the system architecture and full-stack implementation.

- Built scalable APIs for hotel operations and management workflows.
- Developed and connected management interfaces across the application.
- Worked across the PostgreSQL, GraphQL, NestJS, and React stack.

## Contribution

The project required coordinating several operational domains within a shared architecture, with dependable APIs and interfaces supporting each workflow.
