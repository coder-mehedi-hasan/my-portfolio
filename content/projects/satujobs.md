## Project Scope

SatuJobs is an online marketplace that brings employment, freelance work, professional services, and project opportunities into one platform. Employers can look for people to hire, while candidates and freelancers can discover work that matches their needs.

## My Role

I handled the project as a **Full-stack Developer** at **BitPixel Software Solutions**.

- Gathered and clarified requirements directly with the client.
- Took features through full-stack implementation.
- Managed deployment and helped the client through delivery and handover.

## Contribution

SatuJobs was an end-to-end ownership project. Alongside development, I translated client needs into implementable features and supported the product through deployment, giving me experience across both engineering and client communication.
