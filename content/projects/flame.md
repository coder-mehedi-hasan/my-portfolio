## Project Scope

Flame is an AI-powered automation platform for Facebook messages and comments. It helps businesses automate customer interactions using information from their own knowledge bases.

## My Role

I contributed to the retrieval and response-generation pipeline behind the automated conversations.

- Implemented retrieval-augmented generation for knowledge-grounded responses.
- Used vectorization, semantic retrieval, and AI-powered response generation.
- Helped produce context-aware conversations for customer messages and comments.

## Contribution

The work connected business knowledge with semantic search and response generation so automated replies could remain relevant to the customer's conversation.
