## Project Scope

ESPD is a UK-based class-booking platform. After registering, students can choose a subject and teacher, book a suitable time, and review their upcoming classes from a personal dashboard. At the scheduled time, both the student and teacher can launch the class through Google Meet.

Teachers have a dedicated dashboard for managing their availability, reviewing bookings, and joining scheduled classes.

## My Role

I worked on ESPD as a **Backend Developer** at **BitPixel Software Solutions**, with responsibility for API development.

- Developed APIs for the student and teacher booking flows.
- Supported teacher availability and scheduled-class data used by both dashboards.
- Helped connect the booking lifecycle with the class-joining experience.

## Contribution

The work required keeping availability, bookings, and the two user dashboards in sync. I focused on providing a dependable backend contract so the frontend could present the correct class status and actions to students and teachers.
