## Project Scope

StoreOS is a SaaS e-commerce platform that enables businesses to create and manage online stores with flexible product, content, and storefront management.

## My Role

I worked across the full-stack architecture and implementation.

- Developed GraphQL APIs and backend services with NestJS.
- Contributed to database design and data management with MongoDB.
- Built web application features with Next.js.
- Connected backend capabilities with the product and storefront management experience.

## Contribution

My work spanned the application stack, helping turn the platform's store-management requirements into connected APIs, data models, and web interfaces.
