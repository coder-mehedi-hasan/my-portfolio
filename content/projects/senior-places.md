## Project Scope

Senior Places helps individuals and families in the United States find suitable senior-living options. Users can explore care homes, assisted-living facilities, and retirement communities by location or by the criteria that matter to them.

The platform combines geolocation-aware search with a personalized quiz, using a visitor's preferences and location to recommend relevant nearby communities.

## My Role
