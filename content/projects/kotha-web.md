## Project Scope

Kotha Web is the browser-based experience for Kotha, a Bangladesh-based social media platform. The product brings the platform's social and messaging experience to the web, with chat as a central part of the interface.

## My Role

I worked on the product as a **Frontend Developer** at **Kotha Apps & Technologies Ltd.**, focusing on chat integration.

- Integrated the chat experience into the web application.
- Connected the conversation interface with the application's messaging flows.
- Worked on the user-facing behavior needed for a responsive, usable chat experience.

## Contribution

The work strengthened my experience with interactive frontend features where interface state and incoming conversation data need to stay aligned. My focus was making the chat flow feel coherent inside the wider social platform.
